# AAAI-27 OpenReview Submission Steps

Use these files for the AAAI-27 Main Technical Track review submission.

## 1. Confirm Deadline

The official AAAI-27 timetable lists:

- Abstract deadline: July 21, 2026 at 11:59 PM UTC-12.
- Full paper deadline: July 28, 2026 at 11:59 PM UTC-12.
- Supplementary material and code deadline: July 31, 2026 at 11:59 PM UTC-12.

In New York time, the full-paper deadline is July 29, 2026 at 7:59 AM EDT.

## 2. Upload Main Paper

Field: `PDF`

Upload:

```text
papers/meta-routing/aaai2027/submission/AAAI27_Anonymous.pdf
```

This is the anonymous 7-page AAAI-format review PDF.

## 3. Upload Reproducibility Checklist

Field: `Reproducibility Checklist`

Upload:

```text
papers/meta-routing/aaai2027/submission/AAAI27_ReproducibilityChecklist.pdf
```

AAAI-27 requires the completed standalone checklist as a separate upload.

## 4. Upload Code/Data Supplement

Field: `Code and Data Supplement`

Upload:

```text
papers/meta-routing/aaai2027/submission/code_data_supplement.zip
```

This archive includes anonymous source code, experiment scripts, result files,
and instructions needed to assess reproducibility. Do not use an external
repository link during review.

## 5. Leave Optional Supplements Blank Unless Needed

- `Technical Supplement`: leave blank unless adding a separate anonymous PDF.
- `Media Supplement`: leave blank unless adding anonymous images, audio, video,
  or animations.

Reviewers are not required to consult supplementary material, so the main paper
should stand on its own.

## 6. Fill Form Fields

Title:

```text
Learning Compositional Meta-Routing for Agentic Workflows: An Executable Benchmark
```

TL;DR:

```text
We introduce an executable benchmark and budget-aware learned router for deciding when agentic workflows should decompose, retrieve, execute code, delegate, verify, or answer directly.
```

Country of Institutions:

```text
United States
```

License:

```text
CC BY 4.0
```

## 7. Check Policy Acknowledgements

Before checking the acknowledgement boxes, confirm:

- Every author has an up-to-date OpenReview profile with publication name,
  current position, institution-affiliated email, and DBLP URL if available.
- The manuscript, or a substantially similar version, is not under review at
  another archival venue.
- Any relevant simultaneous submissions by any author are cited anonymously as
  under-review work.

## 8. Do Not Upload Camera-Ready Files Now

The AuthorKit `CameraReady2027.*` files are examples/instructions for accepted
papers. For initial AAAI review, submit the anonymous PDF, standalone checklist,
and optional anonymous supplements only.
